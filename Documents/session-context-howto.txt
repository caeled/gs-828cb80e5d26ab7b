Why This Exists
Long sessions compress. When a session gets long enough, Perplexity compacts the earlier turns
— context gets summarized instead of preserved verbatim. When that happens, the AI starts
guessing at things it used to know precisely: paper numbers, Drive IDs, which conversation
something happened in, what you actually said.
This system is the mailbox protocol applied inward. Instead of emailing context across a session
boundary, you load it.
